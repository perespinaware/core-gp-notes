Restoring adds the backup to what's here; it doesn't delete anything.

Where your data lives

  • In this browser, and in a private folder on your own server (outside the website, like your SCA settings). It can't be opened from the web.
  • The public site never contains your answers or your private questions.
  • Your server also keeps hourly copies of the last 40 versions.
  • Anyone who knows your passcode could sync, so keep it private.
